## 🚀 Key Featured Projects

1. **NIDAR — Autonomous Agriculture Precision Drone**
   - Two-drone cooperative workflow: scouting/scanning stressed plants & companion precision spraying
   - Pixhawk Cube Orange + NVIDIA Jetson Nano companion computer, ArduPilot, ROS 2
2. **Off-Road ATV & Go-Kart — Student Motorsport**
   - Tubular spaceframe chassis, double-wishbone suspension, Ackerman steering geometry
   - FOMBI 2K25 National Winners, FOMBI 2K24 Runners-Up
3. **Autonomous Wall Sander Robot — Kinematic Design**
   - Multi-axis reachability & uniform surface pressure kinematics in Autodesk Fusion 360
4. **Siemens MCD Simulation**
   - Physics-accurate contact, multi-body friction, and sensor-actuator mechatronics simulation
5. **ROS 2 & Gazebo — AMR Physics Simulation**
   - Micro-ROS, ROS 2 Humble node architecture, URDF modeling, and PID trajectory control
6. **4WD RC Vehicle — Ackerman Steering & Arduino**
   - Custom Ackerman steering trapezoid mechanism and RF wireless microcontroller control
7. **LPG Gas Leakage Detection System**
   - MQ-05 semiconductor sensor, < 500ms response time, relay automated shut-off
8. **Physics-Engine Robot Dynamics & Joint Force Analysis**
   - Contact dynamics, multi-body joint torque profiles, and actuator sizing
9. **Design and Fluid Flow Analysis of Boeing 747 Aircraft**
   - Full SOLIDWORKS CAD assembly (fuselage, swept tapered wings, stabilizers)
   - Aerodynamic CFD in ANSYS Fluent at cruise (Mach 0.8+, V = 265.5 m/s, 12,192 m altitude)
   - Multi-material comparative FEA structural wing analysis (Aluminium, Titanium, Steel, CFRP)
