About Me

I don't just study engineering.
I build systems.

Final-year Mechanical Engineering student at SRKR Engineering College, Bhimavaram, Andhra Pradesh — working across mechanical design, robotics, autonomous systems, drone engineering, and embedded hardware.

My engineering workflow spans the entire stack: CAD modeling in Siemens NX & SOLIDWORKS, FEA simulation in ANSYS, physics-accurate digital twins in Siemens MCD, ROS 2 autonomous navigation, Gazebo physics simulation, and embedded firmware on ESP32 & Arduino. I've competed in national motorsport events, built autonomous mobile robots, designed custom quadcopter frames, and developed LPG safety systems from concept to hardware.
